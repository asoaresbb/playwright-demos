Legal

How we handle your data, the terms you agree to, and how we keep the service secure.

Privacy policy

Last updated on 2 March 2026

We collect only what we need to run the service: your name, your work email, the files you upload and the activity in your workspace.

What we can't see

Files are encrypted on your device before upload. We store them, but we don't have the keys to read them.

Your rights

  • Ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct data that is wrong.
  • Delete your account and everything in it.

Terms of service

Last updated on 15 January 2026

By creating a workspace, you agree to use Acme Cloud only for files you have the right to share.

Billing

Plans are billed per user, monthly or yearly, in advance. You can cancel at any time, and the workspace stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

Ending your account

After cancelling, you have 30 days to export your files. After that, we delete them permanently.

Security

Last updated on 2 March 2026

Files are encrypted with AES-256 on your device, and keys never leave it unencrypted. All traffic uses TLS 1.3.
