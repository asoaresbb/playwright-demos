Questions

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrades apply straight away and are charged pro rata. Downgrades apply at the end of the billing period.

Do people I share files with need an account?

No. Guests open files from a link and confirm their email with a one-time code. Only your own team needs paid seats.

Where is my data stored?

In data centres in Frankfurt and Zurich. You can choose one of them when you create your workspace.

What happens when the trial ends?

Your workspace becomes read-only for 30 days. Add a payment method to keep sharing, or export your files.
